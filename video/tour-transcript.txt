Scanature turns paper into signed PDFs, on your iPhone or iPad.

You start with what you came to do. Scan a document with the camera. Import a file you were sent. Or go straight to signing one.

The scanner is built for paperwork rather than photographs. It finds the page while you aim, tells you to move closer or hold steady, and takes the shot by itself the moment it is right.

For the paperwork you fill in often, start from a form that is already laid out. Proof of delivery. Vehicle inspection. Expense receipts. Damage reports. Tap a field to fill it in, and sign on the page itself. Your signature is saved, so the next one takes seconds.

Everything you keep is in your library, on the device, and you can lock it behind Face ID.

And that is the part that matters most. There is no account, and nothing is uploaded. Your documents, your signatures and your details stay on your iPhone. It is why the App Store privacy label reads: data not collected.

Scanature. Paperwork, finished where you stand.
