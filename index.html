Scanature

Scan it, fill it in, sign it, send it. Signed paperwork finished on your iPhone or iPad.

What it does

  • Scans paper documents into PDFs with the camera
  • Starts you from a ready-made, fillable form — proof of delivery, vehicle inspection, expense receipt, damage report
  • Imports PDF, JPG and PNG files
  • Adds text, signatures, images, drawings and highlights
  • Reads QR codes and barcodes
  • Turns English scans into text you can select and search
  • Locks the library behind Face ID or your passcode
  • Reads in ten languages, including Arabic and Persian right to left
Your documents stay on your device.

There is no account and no sign-in. Documents, signatures and your profile are written to the app's own storage and sent nowhere, which is why the App Store privacy label says no data is collected.
