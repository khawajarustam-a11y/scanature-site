Get the app

On the App Store, free to try

Tap the badge on an iPhone or iPad and the App Store opens on Scanature. The first three documents are free — no account, and nothing to set up.

Data Not Collected

Your documents never leave your device

No account, no sign-in, nothing uploaded. Scanning, signing and text recognition all run on the device, which is why none of it needs a connection — and why the App Store privacy label for Scanature reads Data Not Collected.
