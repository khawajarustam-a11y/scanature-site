How it works

Four steps, one screen, under a minute.

  1. Point the camera. Scanature finds the page while you aim and captures by itself the moment the shot is right. Or start from a form that is already laid out, or import a PDF you were sent.
  2. Fix anything that needs fixing. Add pages, drag them into order, pull the corners to redo a crop, or rescan the one that came out badly.
  3. Fill it in and sign it. Tap a field to type, tap a signature box to sign. Your signature is saved for next time and can be moved or removed until you are happy with it.
  4. Send it. Share straight to Mail, Messages or Files without opening an editor, or keep it in the library.

Your documents never leave your device

There is no account and no sign-in. Scans, signatures and your profile are written to the app's own storage and sent nowhere. Scanning, editing, signing and text recognition all run on the device, which is why none of it needs a connection.

That is not a promise we ask you to take on trust: the App Store privacy label for Scanature reads Data Not Collected, and Apple holds us to it.
